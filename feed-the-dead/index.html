FEED THE DEAD · an offline pixel tower defense.
All art is drawn in code and all sound is synthesized with the Web Audio API. No network, no tracking. Progress is saved in your browser's localStorage.
No zombies were harmed. They were just very, very full.

How to play

Opening night... of the deadZombies shamble down the aisle toward your kitchen. Turns out they love junk food even more than brains. Feed them until their STUFFED meter fills and they go POP!
Protect the staff lunchesEvery zombie that reaches the kitchen eats lunches (your lives). Lose them all and the restaurant is overrun.
Build stationsPick a food station, then tap a table or a marked floor tile. On touch screens tap once to preview and again to build. With a mouse, one click builds. Tap a station to upgrade, sell or change its targeting.
Picky eatersFoods are tagged MEAT, VEGGIE, SWEET, DRINK or SPICY. Vegans barely touch meat, gym zombies avoid sweets and drinks, hard-hats are picky about meat, and spice fiends love SPICY. Skaters are too fast to catch: only Slushie, Boba and Churro stations can feed them.
MoneyPopped zombies drop coins and each new wave pays a bonus. Calling waves early pays extra. Tip Jars pay out every wave and boost adjacent stations; Espresso Bars make neighbors fire faster. Secret Recipes (the book button) are buffs for the current run.
Keys (optional)1-9, 0 pick a station · R recipes · N next wave · F speed · Space pause · U upgrade · S sell · T target mode · Esc cancel
