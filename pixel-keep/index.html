PIXEL KEEP · an offline pixel tower defense.
All art is drawn in code and all sound is synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API. No network, no tracking. Progress is saved in your browser's localStorage.

How to play

Defend the keepEnemies walk the dirt path toward your castle. Each one that gets through costs lives. Lose them all and the keep falls.
BuildPick a tower from the palette, then tap a grass tile. On touch screens the first tap previews the spot and a second tap on the same tile builds. With a mouse, one click builds.
Upgrade & sellTap a tower to see its stats, upgrade it (up to 3 times), change targeting or sell it for 60% back.
Know your foesFlyers ignore Cannon and Flame. Armored foes resist physical damage (Arrow, Cannon, Sniper and Missile); use Frost, Tesla or Flame. Wraiths resist magic damage, so use physical towers against them.
EconomyKills pay gold, and each new wave pays a bonus. Calling a wave early pays extra. Gold Mines pay out every wave and, once upgraded, boost the damage of adjacent towers. Research (the flask button) gives buffs for the current run.
Keys (optional)1-8 pick a tower · 9 research · N next wave · F speed · Space pause · U upgrade · S sell · T target mode · Esc cancel
